# OpsForge Code of Conduct

OpsForge is committed to fostering an open, inclusive, and respectful environment for all contributors.  
This document defines expected behaviors, unacceptable conduct, and enforcement procedures.

---

## 🤝 Expected Behaviors
- Be respectful and inclusive in all communications.  
- Collaborate constructively and provide helpful feedback.  
- Follow CI/CD hygiene and documentation standards.  
- Respect module boundaries:
  - **BuildMate** → software-only (construction SaaS workflows, compliance logging, project ops).  
  - **DroneOps** → all drone hardware and aerial intelligence (surveying, surveillance, counter‑drone, telemetry).  
- Protect sensitive information and respect contributor privacy.  

---

## 🚫 Unacceptable Conduct
- Harassment, discrimination, or abusive behavior.  
- Unauthorized commercial use of OpsForge modules.  
- Mixing drone hardware references into BuildMate (software-only).  
- Circumventing CI/CD validation or audit logging.  
- Public disclosure of unpatched vulnerabilities.  

---

## ⚖️ Enforcement
- Violations will be reviewed by the OpsForge maintainer.  
- Consequences may include warnings, temporary suspension, or permanent removal from the project.  
- Enforcement decisions are final and guided by fairness and contributor safety.

OpsForge is designed to be a **modular, compliant, and enterprise-ready ecosystem**.

- **BuildMate = software-only.**  
- **DroneOps = all drone hardware/aerial intelligence.**
