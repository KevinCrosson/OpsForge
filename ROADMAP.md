# OpsForge Umbrella Roadmap

This roadmap outlines the strategic direction of OpsForge as a modular ecosystem.  
It enforces strict boundaries between BuildMate (software-only) and DroneOps (hardware/aerial intelligence).

---

## 📅 Short-Term Goals (0–6 months)
- Finalize layered governance and licensing across all modules.  
- Release NetworkAudit with dashboards, packet capture, and CI/CD integration.  
- Expand BuildMate into a SaaS-ready construction workflow platform (software-only).  
- Launch DroneOps YOLOv8 training for aerial detection and telemetry logging.  
- Establish SmartSecurity for centralized surveillance and orchestration.  
- Deploy DeepForensics chain-of-custody workflows and audit trails.  

---

## 📅 Mid-Term Goals (6–18 months)
- Scale BuildMate into a full construction SaaS platform with compliance logging.  
- Expand DroneOps into telemetry, counter-drone, and aerial intelligence modules.  
- Integrate SmartSecurity with enterprise device tracking and orchestration.  
- Enhance DeepForensics with global compliance and forensic attribution.  
- Monetize NetworkAudit dashboards with subscription and Stripe integration.  

---

## 📅 Long-Term Goals (18+ months)
- Position OpsForge for enterprise and educational adoption.  
- Expand BuildMate SaaS workflows globally.  
- Grow DroneOps into a dedicated aerial intelligence and counter-drone platform.  
- Scale DeepForensics into a global compliance suite.  
- Release NetworkAudit cross-platform support beyond Windows-first.
