# OpsForge Commercial Licensing

OpsForge is open-source for collaboration but **closed for profit**.  
All commercial rights are reserved exclusively by **Kevin Crosson**.  
This document defines the process for requesting commercial licensing.

---

## ⚖️ Scope of Licensing
OpsForge consists of multiple modules, each with distinct boundaries:

- **BuildMate** → Construction SaaS platform (software-only: workflows, compliance logging, project ops).  
- **DroneOps** → Aerial intelligence and drone hardware (surveying, surveillance, counter‑drone, telemetry).  
- **SmartSecurity** → Centralized surveillance and orchestration (video, phone tracking, access control).  
- **DeepForensics** → Post‑incident forensic analysis (hidden files, attribution, chain‑of‑custody).  
- **NetworkAudit** → IT/network monitoring suite (packet capture, diagnostics, compliance).  

Commercial licensing applies to **all modules**, but boundaries must be respected:
- BuildMate is strictly software-only.  
- DroneOps is the sole module for drone hardware and aerial intelligence.  

---

## 📜 Licensing Process
Organizations seeking commercial use must:

1. **Submit a request** directly to Kevin Crosson.  
2. **Provide documentation** of intended use and compliance requirements.  
3. **Negotiate licensing terms and fees** with Kevin Crosson.  
4. **Obtain written approval** before deployment.  

Unauthorized commercial use is strictly prohibited.

---

## 🚫 Restrictions
- Commercial use without explicit written approval is forbidden.  
- Redistribution or sublicensing is prohibited.  
- Drone hardware references must remain in **DroneOps** only.  
- BuildMate must remain **software-only**.
