# OpsForge Module-Specific Roadmap

This roadmap provides detailed milestones for each OpsForge module.  
It enforces strict boundaries between BuildMate (software-only) and DroneOps (hardware/aerial intelligence).

---

## 🏗️ BuildMate (Software-Only)
- Short-Term → SaaS workflows, compliance logging, project ops.  
- Mid-Term → Expand into construction SaaS platform.  
- Long-Term → Global adoption as a software-only construction industry platform.  

---

## ✈️ DroneOps (Hardware/Aerial Intelligence)
- Short-Term → YOLOv8 training for drone detection.  
- Mid-Term → Telemetry logging and aerial intelligence expansion.  
- Long-Term → Counter-drone platform with global compliance.  

---

## 🔐 SmartSecurity
- Short-Term → Device tracking and surveillance orchestration.  
- Mid-Term → Enterprise orchestration and integration.  
- Long-Term → Full enterprise rollout with centralized monitoring.  

---

## 🕵️ DeepForensics
- Short-Term → Chain-of-custody workflows and audit trails.  
- Mid-Term → Global compliance and forensic attribution.  
- Long-Term → International adoption as a compliance suite.  

---

## 🌐 NetworkAudit
- Short-Term → Dashboards, packet capture, CI/CD integration.  
- Mid-Term → Monetization via subscription and Stripe integration.  
- Long-Term → Cross-platform release beyond Windows-first.
