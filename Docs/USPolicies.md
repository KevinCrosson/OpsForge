# OpsForge U.S. Policies

This document outlines compliance and legal requirements across the United States, including federal and state laws.  
OpsForge modules (SmartSecurity, DroneOps, DeepForensics, BuildMate, NetworkAudit) must adhere to these standards.

---

## 🇺🇸 Federal Policies
- **DroneOps**
  - FAA Remote ID compliance required for all drones operating in U.S. airspace.
  - Adherence to FAA Part 107 rules for commercial drone operations.
- **SmartSecurity**
  - Employee monitoring must comply with the Electronic Communications Privacy Act (ECPA).
  - Consent forms required for GPS tracking, photo capture, and remote wipe.
- **DeepForensics**
  - Digital evidence handling must follow NIST guidelines for admissibility in federal courts.
- **BuildMate**
  - OSHA compliance for construction safety workflows.
- **NetworkAudit**
  - Federal cybersecurity standards (NIST CSF, CISA guidelines).

---

## 📍 State Policies (Example: Texas)
- **DroneOps.CounterUAS**
  - Texas Government Code §423 prohibits intrusive drone surveillance; evidence logs must be admissible in court.
- **SmartSecurity.PhoneTracking**
  - Texas labor laws require employee consent for monitoring.
- **BuildMate**
  - Texas building codes and safety standards must be enforced.
- **DeepForensics**
  - Chain-of-custody documentation required for admissibility in Texas courts.

---

## ⚖️ Chain-of-Custody
- Immutable logs, timestamps, and encrypted archives required for admissibility in U.S. courts.
- Audit trails must document every action taken on evidence.

---

## 🚀 Strategic Positioning
OpsForge ensures compliance with **federal and state laws** across the United States, making it enterprise- and education-ready.
