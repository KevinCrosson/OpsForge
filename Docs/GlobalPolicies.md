# OpsForge Global Policies

This document outlines compliance and legal requirements across international jurisdictions.  
OpsForge modules must adhere to aviation, privacy, and evidence standards worldwide.

---

## 🌍 Aviation
- **DroneOps**
  - ICAO (International Civil Aviation Organization) standards for UAV operations.
  - Remote ID equivalents in EU, Asia, and other regions.
  - Local airspace restrictions must be respected.

---

## 🌍 Data Privacy
- **SmartSecurity**
  - GDPR compliance for data collection in the EU.
  - CCPA (California) as a global reference for privacy frameworks.
  - Regional privacy laws (APAC, LATAM) must be observed.

---

## 🌍 Digital Evidence
- **DeepForensics**
  - ISO/IEC 27037 standards for evidence preservation.
  - Chain-of-custody documentation for international admissibility.
- **NetworkAudit**
  - International cybersecurity frameworks (ISO/IEC 27001, ENISA guidelines).

---

## 🌍 Construction
- **BuildMate**
  - International building codes and safety standards.
  - Local compliance modules for expansion into EU, Asia, and LATAM markets.

---

## 🚀 Strategic Positioning
OpsForge policies ensure compliance across **global jurisdictions**, enabling adoption by enterprises, governments, and law enforcement worldwide.
