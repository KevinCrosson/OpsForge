# BuildMate Roadmap

BuildMate is the construction industry SaaS platform within OpsForge.  
It manages materials, shipments, compliance, and project operations.

---

## 🔹 Short-Term (0–6 months)
- **Materials**
  - Inventory management for construction materials.
  - Automated ordering and supplier integration.
- **Shipments**
  - Shipment tracking with GPS and status updates.
  - Integration with SmartSecurity for secure delivery verification.
- **Compliance**
  - OSHA/code compliance checklists.
  - Automated reporting into AutoDocs.
- **ProjectOps**
  - CI/CD-style pipelines for project workflows.
  - Contributor onboarding documentation.

---

## 🔹 Mid-Term (6–18 months)
- **Materials**
  - Predictive ordering based on project timelines.
  - Supplier dashboards with analytics.
- **Shipments**
  - Multi-carrier integration for logistics.
  - Automated alerts for delays or theft.
- **Compliance**
  - AI-powered compliance validation.
  - Integration with DeepForensics for incident analysis.
- **ProjectOps**
  - Role-based dashboards for contractors, inspectors, and managers.
  - Integration with DroneOps surveys for site progress validation.

---

## 🔹 Long-Term (18+ months)
- **Materials**
  - Full ERP integration for enterprise resource planning.
- **Shipments**
  - Blockchain-based shipment verification.
  - SaaS subscription model for logistics providers.
- **Compliance**
  - Automated compliance certification exports.
  - Legal-ready audit trails.
- **ProjectOps**
  - Predictive analytics for project risk forecasting.
  - SaaS platform for contractors and enterprises.

---

## 🚀 Strategic Positioning
BuildMate is the **construction arm of OpsForge**, tying together materials, shipments, compliance, and project operations.
