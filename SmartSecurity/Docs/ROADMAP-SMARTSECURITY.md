# SmartSecurity Roadmap

SmartSecurity is the centralized surveillance and orchestration platform within OpsForge.  
It unifies **NetworkAudit**, **DroneOps**, and **BuildMate** into one intelligent security ecosystem.

---

## 🔹 Short-Term (0–6 months)
- Core dashboard for unified monitoring (NetworkAudit logs, DroneOps telemetry, BuildMate compliance).
- Video integration (CCTV/DVR + drone feeds).
- Basic access control (RFID/badge logging).
- AutoDocs integration for compliance reports.

---

## 🔹 Mid-Term (6–18 months)
- AI-powered video analytics (PPE detection, crowd monitoring, suspicious activity).
- Real-time alerts and escalation workflows.
- Counter-drone detection via DroneOps.
- Multi-site support with role-based access.

---

## 🔹 Long-Term (18+ months)
- Full SaaS platform with subscription model.
- IoT expansion (fire, environmental, access gates).
- Predictive intelligence for risk forecasting.
- Compliance validation and exportable audit trails.
- Enterprise integrations (ERP/CRM connectors, APIs).

---

## 🚀 Strategic Positioning
SmartSecurity is the **command center** of OpsForge:  
- **NetworkAudit** → IT/network security feeds.  
- **DroneOps** → aerial surveillance + counter-drone telemetry.  
- **BuildMate** → construction compliance + access control.  
- **AutoDocs** → automated reporting and changelog bundling.
