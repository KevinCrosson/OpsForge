# DroneOps Roadmap

DroneOps is the dedicated aerial intelligence project within OpsForge.  
It provides surveying, surveillance, and counter‑drone capabilities with hardware integration limited to drones.

---

## 🔹 Short-Term (0–6 months)
- **Surveying**
  - Automated site mapping with thermal + RGB imaging.
  - Terrain analysis and progress tracking against CAD/BIM models.
- **Surveillance**
  - Scheduled perimeter patrols with GPS flight paths.
  - Intrusion detection using motion and heat signatures.
- **Telemetry**
  - Log GPS, altitude, and RF signals for each flight.
  - Export telemetry into SmartSecurity dashboards.

---

## 🔹 Mid-Term (6–18 months)
- **Surveying**
  - AI‑powered object detection (YOLOv8) for construction assets and site hazards.
  - Integration with BuildMate for project progress validation.
- **Surveillance**
  - Real‑time video streaming into SmartSecurity.Video.
  - Automated alerts for unauthorized activity.
- **CounterUAS**
  - Remote ID logging for intrusive drones.
  - Flight pattern tracking with timestamped logs.
  - Operator attribution when available.
- **Telemetry**
  - Correlation of drone telemetry with SmartSecurity alerts.

---

## 🔹 Long-Term (18+ months)
- **Surveying**
  - Predictive analytics for construction planning and logistics.
  - SaaS integration for contractors and inspectors.
- **Surveillance**
  - AI‑driven patrol scheduling and risk forecasting.
  - Multi‑drone coordination for large sites.
- **CounterUAS**
  - Advanced detection of spoofed or non‑compliant drones.
  - Evidence bundling for law enforcement (ID, flight path, operator info).
  - Legal compliance with Texas privacy and FAA regulations.
- **Telemetry**
  - Unified telemetry archive with chain‑of‑custody documentation.
  - Integration with DeepForensics for post‑incident analysis.

---

## 🚀 Strategic Positioning
DroneOps is the **hardware arm of OpsForge**, specializing in aerial intelligence.  
It supports BuildMate (construction surveys), SmartSecurity (surveillance + counter‑drone), and DeepForensics (telemetry evidence).
