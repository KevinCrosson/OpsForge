# OpsForge Governance

OpsForge enforces clear governance to ensure contributor clarity, compliance, and ecosystem integrity.  
This document consolidates all governance references and defines contributor responsibilities.

---

## 📖 Governance Overview
OpsForge is structured as a modular ecosystem with strict boundaries:
- **BuildMate** → software-only (construction SaaS workflows, compliance logging, project ops).  
- **DroneOps** → all drone hardware and aerial intelligence (surveying, surveillance, counter‑drone, telemetry).  
- **SmartSecurity** → centralized surveillance and orchestration.  
- **DeepForensics** → forensic attribution, evidence preservation, chain‑of‑custody.  
- **NetworkAudit** → IT/network monitoring, diagnostics, and compliance.  

Governance ensures contributors respect these boundaries and maintain compliance across all modules.

## ⚖️ Contributor Responsibilities
- Respect module boundaries (BuildMate = software-only, DroneOps = hardware/aerial intelligence).  
- Follow CI/CD hygiene and documentation standards.  
- Report vulnerabilities responsibly via [SECURITY-DISCLOSURE.md](SECURITY-DISCLOSURE.md).  
- Adhere to the [CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).  
- Ensure compliance with U.S. and global legal standards.  

---

## 🚫 Prohibited Actions
- Unauthorized commercial use of OpsForge modules.  
- Mixing drone hardware references into BuildMate.  
- Circumventing CI/CD validation or audit logging.  
- Public disclosure of unpatched vulnerabilities.
